# Sources and notices

## Paper source

The Thai prose, equations, mathematical arguments, and bibliography are adapted from the user-supplied paper “แบบจำลองการกำหนดราคาออปชัน: จากบาเชอลิเยถึงการประมาณค่าออปชันแบบอเมริกัน” by Triphop Mahithitarmmatorn. The original PDF is distributed in `downloads/option-pricing-models.pdf`. The website changes presentation and adds an independently implemented model explorer; it does not change the paper's authorship.

The Binomial, Trinomial, and convergence figures are regenerated from the paper's supplied Python scripts. They are deterministic mathematical figures, not generated artwork.

## Runtime and visual assets

React and React DOM are bundled for local use; retained licenses are in `vendor/`. KaTeX renders HTML and MathML equations under its MIT license. Roboto, Roboto Mono, and Noto Sans Thai are packaged locally with their licenses under `assets/fonts/`.

The search icon is Tabler Icons `search` from commit `6d128ed935d4546607b1e4d5d08c8b27bdbe7758`, licensed under MIT. QuantCorner and Quantsera logos and the QuantCorner favicon are owner-approved assets used unchanged; brand rights remain with their respective owners.

The website follows the no-image-generator route. No generated decorative filler was used.
